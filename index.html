Render Studio CAD v1.0.0

A CAD/modeling application with a SketchUp-style tool-first interface and Fusion-style precision underneath.

Choose a tool from a toolbar or menu, then work directly in the 3D viewport. Toolbars and trays can be moved and docked around the workspace.

Keyboard: Space Select, M Move, Q Rotate, S Scale, L Line, R Rectangle, C Circle, P Push/Pull, T Tape/Guide, O Orbit, H Pan, Z Zoom, Delete delete, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Shift+S tool search.
